The action is a short recoil operated tilting barrel design which has been widely copied across the industry and remains the dominant arrangement for service pistols today. The frame is a polymer moulding with steel inserts carrying the slide rails, and the trigger mechanism is a partially pre-cocked striker with an integrated blade safety in the trigger face. Production continues today in several plants and the design has been adopted by many police and military users around the world over the course of four decades of service.
