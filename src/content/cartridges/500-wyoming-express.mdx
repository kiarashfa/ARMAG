The .500 Wyoming Express (.500 WE) is a belted, straight-walled big-bore handgun cartridge that Freedom Arms introduced in 2005 for its Model 83 .500 WE revolver. The article says it is used almost exclusively in revolvers and was designed mainly for hunting.

It is dimensionally similar to the .50 Action Express, but the article says it can be loaded to a higher power level and with heavier bullets, because of a greater maximum chamber pressure and a longer overall cartridge length. It calls it unique among handgun cartridges in being belted, which allows a heavy roll crimp without compromising headspace, unlike the .50 AE. It describes it as less powerful than the .500 S&W Magnum but available in more compact revolvers, as somewhat more powerful than the .500 Linebaugh, .500 JRH, .475 Linebaugh and .454 Casull, and as ballistically comparable to the .500 Maximum, .50-70 Government and .50 Beowulf, which are normally chambered in much larger firearms.

The article says the brass was out of production for several years and is currently available from TII Armory, with loading dies and ammunition.
