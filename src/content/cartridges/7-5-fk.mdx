The 7.5 FK is a bottlenecked, rimless centerfire pistol cartridge the Czech firm FK Brno Engineering
developed between 2009 and 2014, built with an unusually specific design brief: to let a handgun retain
more kinetic energy at 75 to 150 metres than a 9×19mm Parabellum round generates at the muzzle. Reaching
that goal meant a longer, higher-pressure cartridge than existing high-capacity service pistols could
safely chamber, so FK Brno had to build a dedicated platform around it rather than adapt an existing design.

The cartridge first appeared in the all-steel FK BRNO Field Pistol of 2015, which used a patented
recoil-attenuating mechanism to manage the round's pressure and recoil in a handgun-sized frame; a lighter
polymer-framed version, the FK PSD, followed in 2020. Both are multi-calibre designs that can also be
configured for more conventional chamberings, with the 7.5 FK as their signature high-performance option.

All dimensional and pressure figures on this page come from C.I.P.'s own published standard for the
cartridge and are recorded as verified. The two published bullet loads and their velocities come from
Wikipedia's own uncited infobox rather than a clickable primary source and are recorded as estimated.
