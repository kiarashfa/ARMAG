name: "20 gauge"
---

The 20 is the smaller of the two shotgun bores in general use, and the pressure ceiling C.I.P. sets for
it is higher than the 12's: a narrower bore means a smaller area for the gas to work against, so more
pressure is needed for the same result. Shooters who assume the smaller gauge is the gentler one are
half right, because what they feel is recoil, and recoil follows the mass being launched rather than
the pressure behind it.

This is an identity-only entry. No load is recorded, so no page on this site quotes a muzzle velocity
or an energy figure for a 20-gauge arm.
