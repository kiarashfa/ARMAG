id: 44-40-winchester
name: ".44-40 Winchester"
---

The first centre-fire cartridge Winchester made for itself, introduced with the Model 1873 and
adopted almost immediately by Colt for the Single Action Army. That coincidence — one cartridge feeding
both a rifle and a revolver — is the whole reason it mattered: a rider could carry one kind of ammunition
for both arms, which in 1873 was a logistical argument rather than a marketing one.

The case is nominally straight-walled and is not quite. It tapers and then necks slightly, with thin brass
at the mouth, and the combination is famously easy to crumple when seating a bullet by hand.

Its C.I.P. pressure ceiling of 110 MPa is among the lowest on this site, and it is a modern figure set for
antique guns rather than a description of what the 1873 load did.
