FN's response to a 1989 NATO requirement for a personal defence weapon: a cartridge small enough
that a soldier could carry two hundred rounds, fast enough to defeat body armour at a hundred metres, and
mild enough to be fired from something the size of a submachine gun. The case is rebated — its rim is
narrower than its body — which lets a bolt face sized for a smaller cartridge close on a fatter case.

Whether it succeeded depends on which claim is being tested. Against soft armour it does what was asked.
Against the argument that a pistol-calibre carbine was already adequate, it has never fully won, and
adoption outside special-purpose units has been thin.

The single load below carries a bullet mass and no velocity. The article states 716 for the SS190 without
a unit that can be checked against a published energy, and this site records a velocity only when the
figure and the barrel that produced it are both stated.
