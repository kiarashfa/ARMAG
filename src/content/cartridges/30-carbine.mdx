---

Designed in 1940 for a weapon that did not yet exist, against a requirement for something to give
drivers, gunners and radio operators more reach than a pistol without the weight of a rifle. Winchester
built it on the old .32 Self-Loading case, straight-walled and rimless, throwing a 7.1 g round-nosed
bullet at a little under 600 m/s.

It is routinely described as underpowered and routinely compared to the wrong thing. Against the
7.92×33mm Kurz that Germany fielded in the same years it is clearly the lesser cartridge; against the
.45 ACP it replaced in the hands of the people who actually carried it, it roughly triples the muzzle
energy and quadruples the useful range.

The single load below is the service round, and its test barrel is stated — which is the only reason it
appears here at all.
