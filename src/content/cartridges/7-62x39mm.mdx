id: 7-62x39mm
name: "7.62x39mm"
---

The Soviet intermediate round was specified in 1943 against captured German 7.92x33mm ammunition and
the rifle built for it, and it reached service in the SKS before the rifle it is now inseparable from
existed. Its case is strongly tapered, which is what lets a badly fouled or badly made chamber still
extract, and which forces the pronounced curve of every magazine that feeds it.

Bullet diameter is 7.92 mm, the same nominal figure as the 7.62x54mmR and the German 7.92x57mm, all of
which measure their calibre across the lands rather than the grooves. A cartridge called 7.62 firing a
7.92 mm bullet is not a contradiction, and it catches out a great many specification tables.
