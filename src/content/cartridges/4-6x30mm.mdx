id: 4-6x30mm
name: "HK 4.6×30mm"
---

The direct competitor to FN's 5.7×28mm, submitted against the same NATO requirement and losing the
1999 evaluation to it on points that both manufacturers still dispute. It is the smaller of the two by
every measure: a 4.65 mm bullet weighing two grams, in a case five millimetres shorter.

It runs at 400 MPa, which is rifle pressure in a cartridge the size of a fingernail, and that is what buys
the velocity. The bullet is a copper-plated steel cylinder rather than a lead-cored jacketed design — at
this diameter there is no room for a conventional core, and the whole projectile is effectively a
penetrator.

Its C.I.P. datasheet was last revised in 2025, the most recent of any cartridge on this site, which is a
small reminder that these standards are living documents and that a citation without an edition date is
worth very little.
