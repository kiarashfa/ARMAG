Winchester released the commercial version two years before NATO adopted the military one, which is
why a great many people who have never owned a service rifle nonetheless own something chambered for this
case. It is short enough for a compact bolt action, efficient enough that a 24-inch barrel gives up little
to the .30-06, and standardised by C.I.P. at 415 MPa — a limit the surplus military round is not held to.

That last point is the only thing about the pair worth remembering: the chambers accept each other's
ammunition, the pressure specifications differ, and rifles built to the looser one should not be fed the
tighter one's proof loads.
