name: ".277 Fury"
---

SIG Sauer developed this cartridge, marketed under the metric name 6.8×51mm for its military submission,
for the US Army's Next Generation Squad Weapon programme. The programme was launched over concern that
improving body armour was eroding how reliably 5.56×45mm NATO rounds could defeat it, and the Army
selected SIG Sauer in 2022 to build the M7 rifle and M250 automatic rifle around this round for
operational testing beginning in 2024.

What makes the cartridge unusual is its case rather than its bullet diameter. Each case is a hybrid of a
stainless-steel base bonded to a brass body through an aluminium locking washer, a construction chosen to
survive an operating pressure well above what a conventional all-brass case is built for. That pressure —
about 80,000 psi by SAAMI's method, converted here to 551.6 MPa — is far higher than a typical rifle
cartridge, and Wikipedia's own account notes this headroom brings an increased risk of case or firearm
rupture that the hybrid case and matching chamber are engineered against.

None of this entry's dimensions carry an inline citation on the Wikipedia article that is its only source
here, so every figure is recorded as `estimated` rather than `verified`, and no primary SIG Sauer or SAAMI
datasheet has been consulted directly. No free dimensional standard exists for a cartridge this new, and
no photograph illustrates this entry.
