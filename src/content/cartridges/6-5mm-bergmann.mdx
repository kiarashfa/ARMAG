id: 6-5mm-bergmann
name: "6.5mm Bergmann"
---

Bergmann introduced this bottlenecked, semi-rimmed pistol cartridge in 1895 as one of a family of
proprietary rounds built around its own early blowback pistols, sized between the smaller 5mm and larger
8mm Bergmann cartridges the same company offered in different pistol models sold under the same "Bergmann
1896" name. Like its siblings, it was designed and sold as a matched pair with its own pistol rather than
as an independent commercial round adopted across multiple makers' designs.

No C.I.P. or SAAMI dimensional standard exists for a 19th-century proprietary pistol round, so every
figure in this entry is converted from the reference work "Textbook of Automatic Pistols" via the
Wikipedia infobox, and all of them carry `estimated` status. No test-barrel length is published for its one
recorded load, so only bullet mass is recorded and no photograph illustrates this entry.
