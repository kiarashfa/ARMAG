id: 41-action-express
name: ".41 Action Express"
---

The .41 Action Express is a rebated-rim, straight-walled pistol cartridge Evan Whildin, vice
president of Action Arms, designed in 1986 to reproduce the performance of the .41 Magnum's
reduced-power police load in a semi-automatic pistol. Its case was based on the .41 Magnum's, cut
down to fit a 9×19mm Parabellum-sized frame, and its rebated rim let many 9mm pistols be converted
to it with only a change of barrel, mainspring and magazine.

It was overtaken almost at once by the .40 S&W, a shortened 10mm Auto case that also fitted a
9mm-sized pistol and that most ammunition makers backed. With little room left for the very similar
.41 AE, production of both pistols and ammunition was soon phased out, and SAAMI designated it an
inactive cartridge in January 2005.

No C.I.P. or SAAMI free dimensional standard was located for this discontinued round; all
dimensions and pressure here are converted from the figures in Wikipedia's infobox and recorded
as estimated. Three bullet weights are recorded from a handloading data page, but that page's
published velocities are maximum-handload results tied to a specific powder charge rather than
factory-ammunition figures, so no velocity or energy is recorded. No photograph of a pistol
chambered for this cartridge appears on this site.
