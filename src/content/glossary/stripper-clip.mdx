Not a magazine. The clip is a loading aid: it holds five or six rounds in line, the shooter presses it
into guides above an open action, sweeps the cartridges down into the magazine with a thumb, and throws the
clip away.

The Mauser 98, the Mosin-Nagant, the Lee-Enfield and the K31 all load this way. It is why a bolt-action rifle
with an internal magazine can be reloaded almost as quickly as one with a detachable box, and why the
detachable box did not become universal until self-loading rifles made a partly empty magazine worth swapping
out.
