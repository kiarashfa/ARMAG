---
id: blowback-delayed
term: "Delayed blowback"
aka:
  - "Retarded blowback"
short: "Blowback with a mechanical disadvantage — rollers, a lever, friction or gas — slowing the bolt's opening without locking it."
seeAlso:
  - blowback
  - gas-operated
---

A halfway house between simple blowback and a locked breech. The bolt is never actually locked;
something merely makes it hard to open quickly. Four ways of doing that appear on this site, and each is a
different mechanism rather than a variation on one.

Roller-delayed, as in the MP5, drives rollers outwards into recesses through an inclined locking piece, so the
light bolt head can only retreat by driving a heavier bolt body back several times faster. Lever-delayed, as in
the FAMAS, does the same job with a pivoting lever geared against the receiver. Gas-delayed, as in the Heckler
& Koch P7, taps propellant gas into a cylinder that pushes the slide forwards while recoil pushes it back.
Friction-delayed — the Thompson's Blish lock — relies on dissimilar metals resisting sliding under load, and is
the only one of the four that does not really work.
