---
id: overall-length
term: "Cartridge overall length"
aka:
  - "COAL"
  - "L6"
short: "The length of the complete loaded round, base to bullet tip."
seeAlso:
  - case-length
---

C.I.P.'s L6, and the figure that decides whether a cartridge fits a magazine. A round longer than the
magazine well cannot be fed from it however well it fits the chamber, which is a real constraint on how heavy a
bullet a given rifle can use.

It is a maximum rather than a specification. An ammunition maker seats the bullet where it likes within that
limit, and the same cartridge loaded with two different bullets will have two different overall lengths.
