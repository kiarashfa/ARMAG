---
id: en-bloc-clip
term: "En bloc clip"
short: "A clip that is loaded into the magazine along with the cartridges and stays there until the last round is fired."
seeAlso:
  - stripper-clip
---

The difference from a stripper clip is that this one goes in. The clip is part of the feed system:
rounds are pushed up out of it by the magazine follower, and when the last one is chambered the empty clip is
ejected — in the M1 Garand's case upwards, with a metallic ring that is one of the best-known sounds in
firearms.

The consequence is that the rifle cannot be topped up. Fire three rounds from an eight-round clip and the only
ways to reload are to fire the other five or to eject a partly full clip and lose whatever is in it. That
constraint, more than anything else, is why the M14 which replaced the Garand has a detachable box magazine.
