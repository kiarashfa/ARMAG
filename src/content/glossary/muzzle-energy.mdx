---
id: muzzle-energy
term: "Muzzle energy"
short: "The kinetic energy of a projectile as it leaves the muzzle: one half the mass times the velocity squared."
seeAlso:
  - ballistic-coefficient
  - trajectory
---

A calculation, not a measurement. Every energy figure on this site is computed from a stored bullet
mass and a stored velocity, which is why an energy is never recorded as a fact in its own right — storing it
would create a second copy of a derived number, free to drift away from the inputs it came from.

Because velocity is squared, energy is dominated by it: doubling the mass doubles the energy, doubling the
velocity quadruples it. That is the whole argument for small, fast cartridges.

What muzzle energy is not is a measure of effect. This site publishes energy, momentum and retained velocity
because they are physics, and publishes no damage, lethality or stopping-power figure of any kind, because
those are not.
