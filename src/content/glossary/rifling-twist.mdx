---
id: rifling-twist
term: "Rifling twist"
aka:
  - "Twist rate"
short: "The distance in which the rifling makes one complete turn, and so how fast it spins a bullet."
seeAlso:
  - trajectory
---

Recorded here in millimetres per turn, because that is how European standards and most military
documents state it: a twist of 178 mm means one full rotation in 178 mm of barrel. English-language sources
usually write the same thing as a ratio, 1 in 7 inches.

The rate has to match the bullet. A longer, heavier projectile needs a faster twist to stay point-forward; too
slow and it tumbles, too fast and a thin-jacketed bullet can come apart in flight. The M16's change from a
1-in-12-inch twist to 1-in-7 between the A1 and the A2 was made to stabilise the longer SS109 bullet, and it is
why the two rifles prefer different ammunition.
