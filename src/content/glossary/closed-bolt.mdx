---
id: closed-bolt
term: "Closed bolt"
short: "A firing cycle in which the bolt is already forward and locked between shots; only the hammer or striker moves when the trigger is pulled."
seeAlso:
  - open-bolt
---

The normal arrangement for a rifle or a pistol, and the less common one for a submachine gun. The
round is chambered and the breech shut; pulling the trigger releases only a hammer or a striker. Almost nothing
moves, so almost nothing disturbs the aim.

The price is heat. A closed chamber holds a cartridge against a hot barrel, and in a weapon firing long
automatic bursts that risks the round igniting on its own. Designs that fire automatically from a closed bolt
accept that risk because their rates and their duty cycles are lower than a machine gun's.
