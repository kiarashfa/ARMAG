Izhmash absorbed the neighbouring Izhmekh plant and was renamed in 2013, taking the surname of the
designer whose rifles had carried Izhevsk output for six decades. The concern is the current producer
of the AK-12 and of the civilian Saiga and Baikal lines. No arm on this site is yet attributed to it:
everything here that came out of Izhevsk predates the renaming, so the model list below is empty on
purpose rather than unfinished.
