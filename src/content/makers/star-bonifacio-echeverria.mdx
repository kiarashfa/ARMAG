Founded in 1905 by brothers Julian and Bonifacio Echeverria in Spain's Basque region, Star
Bonifacio Echeverria became one of the country's principal pistol and submachine gun
manufacturers through the twentieth century, trading under the Star brand name. The company went
bankrupt and ceased operations in 1997.
