Carl Walther GmbH was founded in 1886 by gunsmith Carl Walther in Zella-Mehlis, Germany, and now operates
from Ulm. It has built pistols, rifles and sporting arms across more than a century, including the PP and
PPK pocket pistols, the P38 and P99 service pistols, and rimfire sporting rifles, alongside optics and
airguns. The company is part of the PW Group and its American arm, Walther Arms, Inc., distributes and
manufactures under the Walther name in the United States.
