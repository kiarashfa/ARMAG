A Miami company set up in 1985 to make inexpensive blowback pistols, and one of the very few
firearms manufacturers whose products are better known from legislation than from shooting. Its output was
cheap to produce, cosmetically aggressive and sold in large numbers; the company closed in 2001. This site
records what it built and the specifications of those arms, and leaves the surrounding argument to
sources equipped to have it.
