Karl Brügger and Heinrich Thomet founded this Thun, Switzerland firm in 1991. Known commercially as B&T,
it built a reputation among military and law-enforcement buyers for compact submachine guns and
suppressors before broadening into rifles and accessories, and it remains a comparatively small,
specialist manufacturer supplying customers worldwide.
