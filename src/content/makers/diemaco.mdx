Diemaco (1984) Inc., a division of Héroux-Devtek Inc. based in Kitchener, Ontario, held the
Canadian government's designation as its "centre of excellence for small arms" from 1974 until
it was sold and renamed Colt Canada in 2005, without ever being government-owned. Under licence from Colt, it built the C7 and C8 rifle
family for the Canadian Armed
Forces and for export customers including the Netherlands and Denmark, establishing the
AR-15-derived, Diemaco-improved rifle pattern that Colt Canada continues to produce.
