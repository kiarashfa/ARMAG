Bushmaster Firearms International is an American firearm maker, the successor of Gwinn Firearms, which Mack Gwinn Jr. founded on his return from the Vietnam War. After a bankruptcy it was bought by Richard Dyke in 1976 and moved from Bangor to Windham, Maine, and Quality Products Company acquired it in 1990. Its product line has revolved around semi-automatic pistol and rifle variants of the AR-15 design, and the Bushmaster trademarks were bought in 2020 in the Remington Outdoor Company bankruptcy auction.

The company is recorded here for the Arm Pistol, a design that predates its AR-15 line. No founding year is given in the source used, so none is shown.
