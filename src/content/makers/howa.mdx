name: "Howa Machinery, Ltd."
country: japan
---

Founded in 1907 near Nagoya, Howa built machine tools and, from the 1960s, licensed AR-pattern rifles for
the Japan Self-Defense Forces before designing its own Type 89 assault rifle. The firm remains a diversified
manufacturer of machinery and construction equipment alongside its firearms business, which is Japan's
sole domestic source of military rifles.
