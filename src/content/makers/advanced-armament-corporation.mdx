Founded in 1994 and based in West Columbia, South Carolina, Advanced Armament Corporation (AAC)
built its business primarily around sound suppressors before expanding into complete firearms,
including the .300 AAC Blackout cartridge it developed and the Honey Badger carbine built around
it. The company operates as a subsidiary of JJE Capital Holdings, LLC.
