Servicios Industriales de la Marina S.A., known as SIMA or SIMA PERU, is a Peruvian shipyard, a state-owned company created in 1950 on the base of the Industrial Department of the Navy Dockyard, continuing the activities of the Naval Factory founded in Callao in 1845. It serves mainly the Peruvian Navy as well as domestic and foreign private clients. It is recorded here as the maker of the MGP series of submachine guns. No company mark was found that could be used, so none is shown.
