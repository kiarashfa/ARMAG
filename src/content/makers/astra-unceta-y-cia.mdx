Astra-Unceta y Cia was a Spanish handgun maker founded on 17 July 1908 as Esperanza y Unceta by Juan Esperanza and Pedro Unceta, in Eibar, the centre of the Basque arms industry. It moved to Guernica in 1913, adopted the Astra trade name in 1914, became Unceta y Compañía in 1925 and took its final name in 1953. It continued making handguns for the Spanish government and export markets until it filed for bankruptcy in 1997.

The company is recorded here for its Mauser C96-pattern pistols of the 1920s and 1930s, which it exported widely. No free source gives a usable company mark, so none is shown.
