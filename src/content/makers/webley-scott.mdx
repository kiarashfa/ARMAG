A Birmingham gunmaking firm founded in 1790, Webley & Scott became the dominant supplier of top-break
service revolvers to the British armed forces from the late nineteenth century, culminating in the .455
Webley Mk VI carried through both World Wars. The company also built shotguns and air pistols, and it
continues to trade in the airgun market today.
