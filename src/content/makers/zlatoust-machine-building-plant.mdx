Founded in 1939 in Zlatoust, in the Chelyabinsk region of the Ural Mountains, the Zlatoust
Machine-Building Plant is a Russian mechanical-engineering company producing small arms and other
machinery. It is best known in firearms circles as the manufacturer of the PP-91 Kedr submachine
gun for Russian law-enforcement and security agencies.
