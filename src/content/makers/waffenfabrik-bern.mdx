name: "Waffenfabrik Bern"
country: switzerland
---

The Swiss federal arms factory, founded at Bern in 1871 and the maker of every service rifle the
country issued for more than a century. Its output is unusual on this site in that almost none of it was
exported: a militia system in which every serving man keeps his rifle at home produces a domestic market
large enough to justify a state factory and a standard of finish that a competitive export market would not
pay for. The works were later absorbed into the federal defence group now called RUAG.
