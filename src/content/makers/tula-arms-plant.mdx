name: "Tula Arms Plant"
country: russia
---

Peter the Great ordered an arms factory at Tula in 1712 and it has been making weapons ever
since, which makes it the oldest manufacturer on this site by more than a century. Imperial muskets, the
Mosin–Nagant, the Tokarev pistol, the Maxim and a long line of Soviet designs all came from it. Arms
attributed to Tula here are those actually built there; Soviet production was routinely split across
several plants, and a Mosin–Nagant may as easily be Izhevsk's.
