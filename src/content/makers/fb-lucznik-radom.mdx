Fabryka Broni "Łucznik" – Radom traces its origin to the Radom Arms Factory founded in 1922,
which produced small arms for interwar and postwar Poland, including under state ownership
through the communist period. It continues as a Polish state-affiliated arms manufacturer, now a
subsidiary of the Polska Grupa Zbrojeniowa defence conglomerate, producing pistols, submachine
guns and other firearms for Polish and export markets.
