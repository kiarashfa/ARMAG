Saco Defense was the Saco, Maine ordnance division of the Maremont Corporation, and was the
principal manufacturer of the M60 machine gun for most of its production run. No free source
gives a founding date for the division itself, and Wikipedia's own article on the M60 names
Saco Defense as a manufacturer without further biography, so no founding year is recorded
here. Production of the M60 family later passed to other contractors, including U.S. Ordnance.
