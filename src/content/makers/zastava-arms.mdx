Zastava Arms is a Serbian and formerly Yugoslav firearms maker based in Kragujevac, Serbia, and among the leading producers of firearms in the Balkans. It dates its foundation to the production of cannon and howitzers at Kragujevac on 27 October 1853, and it exports to over forty countries. Its products include the Zastava M70, a variant of the Kalashnikov rifle.
