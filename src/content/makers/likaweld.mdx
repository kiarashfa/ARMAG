Likaweld was a machine tools factory in Zagreb, Croatia, also called the First Croatian Weapons Factory (Prva Hrvatska Tvornica Oružja, PHTO), and is now bankrupt. It is recorded here as the maker of the Zagi M-91 submachine gun. Wikipedia has no article on the company, so no founding year is given, and no company mark was found.
