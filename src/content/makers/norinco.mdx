China North Industries Corporation was established in 1980 as the trading and export arm of the
People's Republic's ordnance industry, consolidating the output of numbered state arsenals that had
until then reached foreign buyers only through the state itself. Much of what it exports was designed
and first built decades before the corporation existed, so an entry attributed to it here can carry an
introduction year earlier than the company's own founding date: the arsenal that made the arm in 1956
was absorbed, not replaced.
