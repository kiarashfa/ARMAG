Founded at Ithaca, New York in 1880 by a partnership that included two of Leroy Smith's sons,
and a maker of shotguns for its whole existence rather than a maker of military arms that also sold
sporting guns. Its side-by-side doubles and, later, the bottom-ejecting Model 37 pump were its staples.
The company has changed hands and locations several times since 1980; the name is still in use in New
York state.
