name: "Fabrique d'armes Émile et Léon Nagant"
country: belgium
---

Brothers Émile and Léon Nagant founded this Liège firm in 1859. It is best remembered for two designs
that outlived the company itself by decades in Russian and Soviet service: the Mosin-Nagant rifle, on
which Léon Nagant contributed elements of the bolt design, and the M1895 gas-seal revolver he designed
alone. The firm closed in 1931.
