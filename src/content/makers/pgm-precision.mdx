PGM Précision was founded in 1991 by Gilles Payen, Alain Gonnet and François Morier in Poisy,
Haute-Savoie, and builds bolt-action sniper rifles, ammunition and related accessories. Its rifles have
armed French military and police units and several other national forces, competing at the high end of
the factory sniper-rifle market against makers including Accuracy International and Sako.
