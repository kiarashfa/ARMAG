Originally Lyttelton Engineering Works, this Centurion firm built the Vektor R4 — South Africa's Galil-derived
service rifle — and remains the country's principal small-arms and armoured-vehicle manufacturer as part
of the state-owned Denel group.

No free source gives this company's founding date independent of its later corporate name change, so that
field is left out here.
