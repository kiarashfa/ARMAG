The Cx4 Storm is a semi-automatic pistol-calibre carbine Beretta introduced in 2003, built around a single
practical idea: letting an owner of a Beretta pistol carry one magazine type for two different firearms. It
accepts magazines from the Beretta 92/96, 8000 "Cougar" and Px4 pistol families through swappable magwell
sleeves and release buttons, so a shooter equipped with, say, a 92FS in 9×19mm can feed the carbine from
the same magazines rather than stocking a second pattern.

It is a straight blowback design with a Picatinny rail for optics and a tri-rail forend for lights, lasers
and grips, and its controls — fire selector, charging handle, ejection port and magazine release — can all
be switched to the left side for a left-handed shooter. Beretta offers the Cx4 in 9×19mm Parabellum, 9×21mm,
.40 S&W and .45 ACP, the last of these restricted to the 8000 "Cougar" magazine's eight-round capacity
because no larger Beretta pistol magazine exists in that calibre.

A select-fire submachine-gun variant, the Mx4 Storm, shares the Cx4's mechanism and magazine-compatibility
concept adds automatic fire, uses a shorter 312mm barrel, and is most commonly seen with a 30-round 9mm
magazine compatible with the Beretta 92. It uses the same action and operating system, so it is recorded
here as a variant, with the infobox figures given for it.
