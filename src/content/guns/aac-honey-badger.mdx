The AAC Honey Badger is an AR-pattern personal defence weapon Advanced Armament Corporation designed
primarily for suppressed use, chambered in .300 AAC Blackout. AAC's founder, Kevin Brittingham,
wanted a weapon with the ease of use of an AR-15 and the portability of an MP5, and the Honey Badger
pairs a standard M4 upper and lower receiver with a short barrel, a very short direct-impingement
gas system, a fast rifling twist, a large detachable suppressor and a proprietary buffer tube and
two-pronged collapsible stock. Heavy subsonic .300 Blackout ammunition makes it quieter still.

Its fire controls, manipulation and magazines match the M16 family's, which suits military users
already issued that rifle. AAC left the rifle business in 2013 to concentrate on suppressors;
Brittingham founded a new company, Q, LLC, in 2017, which developed and sold an improved Honey
Badger until production ended in 2020.
