The AK-104 is a short-barrelled carbine version of the AK-103 rifle, one of the AK-100 family
Mikhail Kalashnikov designed in 1994. It is chambered in 7.62×39mm, the cartridge of the original
AK-47 and AKM, and feeds from any standard 7.62×39mm AK-pattern magazine.

Its 314 mm barrel is common to the other carbines of the series, the AK-102 and AK-105. Unlike the
shorter, skeleton-stocked AKS-74U it has a solid, side-folding polymer stock, though it keeps a
muzzle booster derived from the AKS-74U's. Documented users include Russia's Federal Protective
Service, Syrian special mission forces and Thailand's Directorate of Logistics.
