The PB, formally the Pistolet Besshumnyy ("silent pistol") and also known by its GRAU index 6P9, is
an integrally suppressed, simple-blowback pistol A.A. Deryagin designed on the basis of the Makarov
pistol. Its suppressor comes in two parts: the pistol can be carried and fired with only the rear
section in place, when it sounds much like an ordinary Makarov, and the front section, carried in a
compartment of a purpose-made holster, is fitted before use. Because the suppressor covers the front
of the barrel, the slide is too short to hold the return spring, which sits in the grip and acts on
the slide through a long lever.

The Izhevsk Mechanical Plant built it from 1967, chambered in the same 9×18mm Makarov cartridge
and using the same 8-round magazine as the standard PM service pistol. It armed the KGB and later
FSB, Soviet Army reconnaissance troops, and the Russian Airborne Forces, and production continued
under the Kalashnikov Concern after Izhevsk Mechanical Plant merged into it in 2013.
