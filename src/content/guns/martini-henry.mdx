The Martini–Henry replaced the Snider–Enfield, itself a muzzle-loader hastily converted to fire a
metallic cartridge, with a rifle designed from the outset as a breechloader. Its falling-block
action combined Henry O. Peabody's dropping breech block, reworked by the Swiss engineer Friedrich
von Martini into a hammerless design cocked by the underlever itself, with polygonal rifling by the
Scottish gunmaker Alexander Henry. Pulling the underlever down and forward tips the block clear of
the chamber for loading and draws the striker spring back in the same motion; there is no separate
hammer to cock and no magazine to load — one round at a time, by hand.

It entered British service in 1871 and stayed there, in one mark or another, for 47 years, fighting
through the colonial campaigns of the late Victorian empire, including Isandlwana and Rorke's Drift
during the Anglo-Zulu War. Early production used a rolled brass-foil cartridge case that expanded
under firing pressure until it stuck or tore in the chamber, jamming the action; the fix, a stronger
drawn-brass case paired with a longer loading lever for more forceful extraction, arrived on the
Mk IV. Ottoman forces used near-identical rifles built under contract by the Providence Tool Company
in Rhode Island, and Romania bought a further batch from Steyr in Austria-Hungary — production the
Royal Small Arms Factory at Enfield, already at capacity rearming British troops, could not itself
supply. The design was eventually superseded by the bolt-action, magazine-fed Lee–Metford, and
converted survivors chambered for smaller-bore cartridges were reissued under other names, among
them the Martini–Enfield and Martini–Metford, rather than as the Martini–Henry itself.

The same falling-block action, retooled by W. W. Greener decades later for a proprietary shotshell
that no other gun could chamber, became the Greener Prison Shotgun issued to colonial prison and
police services; the change of cartridge family and role makes it a separate design built on the
same mechanism rather than a configuration of this one.
