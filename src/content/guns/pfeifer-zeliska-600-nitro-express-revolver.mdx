The Zeliska is an Austrian single-action revolver in .600 Nitro Express, produced by Pfeifer Waffen. It may be the largest handgun in the world apart from the Giant 1859 28mm Remington revolver, at about 6 kilograms and 55 centimetres in length, with the cylinder alone weighing 2 kilograms; the weight helps control recoil. The infobox dates the design to 1995 and production to 2001.

It is described as largely based on the Colt Single Action Army, and like that revolver it is loaded through a gate on the right of the cylinder. The cylinder holds five rounds, either .600 Nitro Express or .458 Winchester Magnum. Its .600 Nitro Express load is given as a 900-grain (58.32 g) bullet at 1,516 ft/s, against 1,850 to 2,050 ft/s typical of 28-inch rifle barrels chambered in the cartridge, with a muzzle energy of 4,592 foot pounds. No barrel length is stated for that velocity, so no load is recorded for it. Decorative features include a gold-plated hammer, cylinder pivot and action and a gold-filled inscription carrying the company's address.
