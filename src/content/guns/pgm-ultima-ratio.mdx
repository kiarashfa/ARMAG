The Ultima Ratio is a French bolt-action sniper rifle built by PGM Précision since 2000, chambered primarily
for 7.62×51mm NATO but offered with barrels for several other rounds as well. Its name is drawn from ultima
ratio regum, "the final argument of kings", the phrase Louis XIV had engraved on his cannon; the rifle
competes commercially against the Accuracy International Arctic Warfare and Sako TRG lines at the high end
of the factory sniper-rifle market.

This entry covers the Intervention configuration, the longest-barrelled of the family, alongside three
variants that share its action and chambering options but differ in barrel length and mass: the shorter
Commando I and Commando II, and an Integral Silencieux configuration built around a shortened barrel
paired with an integral suppressor rather than a conventional muzzle device. Beyond 7.62×51mm NATO, PGM
has chambered the platform for .300 Savage, 7mm-08 Remington, .260 Remington and 6.5mm Creedmoor, along
with the target rounds 6.5×47mm Lapua and 6mm BR Norma, all recorded here as alternative chamberings of
the same rifle.
