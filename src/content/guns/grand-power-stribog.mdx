The Grand Power Stribog is a blowback-operated pistol-caliber platform the Slovak manufacturer
Grand Power has produced since 2016, entering the market first as the SP9A1, a large-format
semi-automatic pistol chambered in 9×19mm Parabellum with a full-length Picatinny top rail and a
threaded, shrouded barrel. This entry describes that base pistol configuration.

Grand Power has since expanded the platform into a wider family sharing the same basic blowback
mechanism and receiver but differing in barrel length, stock and chambering: longer-barreled,
stocked configurations intended as pistol-caliber carbines or select-fire submachine guns for
military and law-enforcement customers, and additional chamberings beyond 9×19mm including .380
ACP, .45 ACP, 10mm Auto, 7.62×25mm Tokarev, .22 Long Rifle and 6mm Flobert. Those longer and
differently chambered configurations are distinct enough in size and role that they are not
modelled as spec rows on this page.

Mass, length and barrel length on this page come from Wikipedia's infobox, which carries no
inline citation for any of them, so all are recorded as estimated rather than verified and
reflect the shortest published SP9A1 pistol configuration specifically. No manufacturer
specification sheet was reachable for this entry. No published twist rate was found, and no
cyclic rate is recorded because this base configuration is semi-automatic only.
