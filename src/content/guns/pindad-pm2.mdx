The Pindad PM2 (Pistol Mitraliur 2, "Submachine Gun 2") is a blowback-operated submachine gun built
by the Indonesian state arsenal PT Pindad and in service since 2006, chambered in 9×19mm Parabellum.
It is based on Pindad's SS2 rifle, with a similar receiver, grip and folding stock, and was intended
for forest-guard authorities and law enforcement; Pindad designed it to exceed the performance of
the Heckler & Koch MP5.

It was first shown abroad at a 2009 defence exhibition in Bangkok. It serves with the Indonesian
Army and Kopassus, and Timor-Leste's national police bought 75 in 2012. All versions have fixed iron
sights and a MIL-STD-1913 rail for optics: the V1 with a folding stock, the V2 with an integral
suppressor, and the V3 with a forward grip.
