The AK-102 is a shortened carbine version of the AK-101 rifle, one of the AK-100 family Mikhail
Kalashnikov designed in 1994. It is chambered in 5.56×45mm NATO rather than a Soviet or Russian
service cartridge, and it is made exclusively for export.

With the AK-104 and AK-105 it forms the carbine half of the family, a middle ground between the
full-length rifles and the more compact AKS-74U. It has a stamped-steel receiver, a shock-resistant
side-folding polymer stock, a reinforced-fibreglass magazine and a muzzle booster derived from the
AKS-74U's. Documented users include Indonesian government forces, the Royal Malaysian Navy's PASKAL
special forces and Thailand's Volunteer Defense Corps.
