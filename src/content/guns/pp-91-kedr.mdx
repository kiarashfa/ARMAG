The PP-91 Kedr is a simple-blowback submachine gun Yevgeny Dragunov, better known for the SVD
sniper rifle, designed in the 1970s, though it did not enter production until 1994, after the
Soviet Union's collapse. The Zlatoust Machine-Building Plant manufactures it, chambered in the
same 9×18mm Makarov cartridge as the standard Soviet and Russian service pistol, and it has been
issued to Russia's interior ministry police (MVD), drug-control service (FSKN) and prison service
(FSIN) as a compact weapon for personnel who need more firepower than a pistol but not a full-size
rifle.

A related design, the PP-9 Klin, shares the Kedr's general layout but uses a delayed-blowback action
and a more powerful loading of the same cartridge for improved ballistics; because that changes the
weapon's operating system, it is not modelled as a variant of this entry.
