The Bushmaster Arm Pistol was a 5.56×45mm NATO firearm produced by the Gwinn Firearms Company and later by Bushmaster Firearms. It was a new design, with a rotating bolt and a long-stroke gas piston similar to those of the AK-47, though it used some AR-15 parts and took STANAG magazines. It has been described as a bullpup pistol, a personal defence weapon and a carbine, and it can be seen as either a long pistol or a compact carbine rifle.

Wikipedia's infobox gives production from 1972 to 1990, while its text says production of the pistol ended in 1988 and of the rifle in 1991, after Quality Products Company acquired Bushmaster the previous year; the two statements do not agree, so only the start year is recorded. No other users or variants are known beyond the pistol and rifle versions.

Gaps on this page: weight, length and barrel length come from the infobox, uncited there, so are estimated. No fire modes are recorded, because no source states them.
