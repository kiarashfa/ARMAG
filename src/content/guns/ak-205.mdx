The AK-205 is a 5.45×39mm carbine, a modernised version of the AK-105, which is itself a shortened carbine version of the AK-74M. It was originally known as the AK-105M and was renamed AK-205 in 2018. It belongs to the AK-200 series, alongside the AK-200, AK-201, AK-202, AK-203 and AK-204, and the AK-202, AK-204 and AK-205 are very similar in design, differing only in chambering, barrel length and magazine type. The Kalashnikov Concern is the manufacturer.

Development stopped around 2011 and resumed around 2016, and the rifle was introduced in 2018, with orders reported from Rosgvardia in that year. The gas-operated long-stroke action and rotating bolt are those of the AK-74 line. The differences it lists are rail-mounted fittings and ergonomics: Picatinny rails on the receiver cover and on the upper and lower handguards, with an optional quad-rail system for optics, lasers, lights, foregrips and bipods; a safety lever with an extended shelf for the trigger finger; and a hinged receiver cover held by a retaining lever at the rear to keep the zero. The selector gives single shots or full automatic. A birdcage-type hybrid flash hider with compensator features sits at the muzzle, and the stock folds and telescopes into four positions and also holds the cleaning rod.

It feeds from a 30-round box or a 60-round casket magazine.
