The APC308 is the 7.62×51mm NATO member of B&T's Advanced Police Carbine family, previewed in October
2016 for release early the following year. B&T presented it as the model that completed the family,
giving a full-power rifle the same ergonomics and controls as the pistol-calibre APC9 and the 5.56mm
APC556, and like the APC556 it is gas-operated, with a short-stroke piston, rather than blowback.

The maker's datasheet lists it as semi-automatic only, in two versions: a carbine with a 330 mm barrel
and a designated-marksman version with a 480 mm barrel, both cold-hammer-forged in Germany with a
one-in-twelve-inch twist chosen to suit a wide range of bullet weights. The upper receiver is machined
from solid alloy with its top and bottom rails cut into it rather than bolted on, the gas block has a
setting for use with a suppressor, and the rifle feeds from SR-25-pattern magazines. Its folding stock
folds to the right and the rifle can be fired with it closed.

Every figure on this page comes from B&T's own datasheet, read through a permanent archived copy. Its
mass figures are stated with a magazine fitted. The datasheet gives no muzzle velocity, and no photograph
of this model with a free licence could be found.
