The Beretta 93R is a short-recoil-operated machine pistol Beretta developed between 1977 and
1979, mechanically similar to the established Beretta 92 pistol but adding select fire, a ported
barrel, a collapsible angled foregrip ahead of the trigger guard and a detachable folding shoulder
stock. It was built for Italy's Polizia di Stato and Carabinieri special-operations units, formed
during the "Years of Lead" period of domestic terrorism in the late 1970s, which needed a
concealable pistol capable of controlled automatic fire.

Rather than full automatic fire, the 93R restricts its select-fire mode to three-round bursts, and
the ported barrel, the foregrip and, when fitted, the stock steady it for burst fire. Unlike the
other Berettas of the 90 series it is single-action only and has no decocker. Beyond its original
Italian users, it was adopted by other police and military forces that needed a concealable weapon
capable of rapid fire.

Mass, length, barrel length, cyclic rate and effective range on this page come from Wikipedia's
